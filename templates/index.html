System Architecture

📡

Data Ingestion

Loads xView GeoJSON annotations and GeoTIFF images. Performs reproducible image-level train/val split at 80/20.

✂️

Data Transformation

Sliding-window chipping (512×512, stride 364). Albumentations augmentation pipeline for training chips.

🧠

Model Training

YOLO11m pretrained weights fine-tuned on xView for 50 epochs with mosaic, copy-paste, and rotation augmentation.

🔍

Tiled Inference

Large images divided into overlapping tiles. All detections merged into global coordinates with batched NMS deduplication.
